The Demonstrator · Company-led design study

A grand tourer, as a point of departure.

Front-mid-engine. Rear-wheel drive.
Composed proportions.

Design direction

Composed, not aggressive.

A luxury GT: front-mid-engine architecture, rear-wheel drive, grand-touring proportions, distinctive contemporary detailing, and a sophisticated interior direction. Neither retro nor futuristic for its own sake, and free of generic hypercar vents, wings, and splitters.

Why begin here

  • To explore the relationship between design and engineering.
  • To establish a coherent point of view.
  • To make the company's philosophy tangible.
  • To give prospective customers a meaningful example of the approach.

A point of departure

The demonstrator expresses the Commonwealth approach in a single automobile. It is a reference point for what follows, not a rigid template — every commission is developed in its own right.
