Ownership

Created with you. Supported beyond delivery.

Your AD remains your primary point of contact, giving continuity between the original vision, the finished automobile, and its care.

Continuing support

Support, for the life of the car.

  • Maintenance guidance and coordination
  • Repair support
  • Technical advice
  • Parts sourcing
  • Preservation of the automobile's records
  • Consideration of future modifications, assessed through an engineering process rather than automatically approved
  • Coordination with appropriate specialists where necessary

A lasting relationship

The relationship continues after delivery.

Commonwealth stays involved in the care of the automobile. We coordinate maintenance and repairs, help source parts, keep the car's records, and remain available for technical questions as long as the car is with you.

Personal touches

  • The watchA bespoke watch inspired by your automobile, designed around your commission rather than a universal template, with restrained branding.
  • A second giftChosen around your vehicle and how it will be used.
  • The first anniversaryA personalized metal keepsake and a defined anniversary inspection of the car.

Common questions

Ownership, plainly.

How long do you support the car?

Support is part of the relationship, not a separate tier. We remain available for guidance, repairs, parts, and records, and we stay involved in the car's care over time.

Does ownership include a maintenance plan?

We coordinate maintenance and repairs rather than offering a fixed package, so care is arranged around your car and how you use it.

Can the car be changed after delivery?

Future modifications are considered through an engineering process rather than automatically approved, so any change is assessed properly.

Who do I deal with over time?

Your AD remains your primary point of contact, giving continuity between the original vision, the finished automobile, and its care.
