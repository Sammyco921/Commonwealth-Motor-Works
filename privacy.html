Privacy

Your privacy, respected.

This policy explains what Commonwealth Motor Company collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send.

If you complete the enquiry form, we receive the details you provide: your name, your email address, your preferred contact method, and the answers you write about the automobile you have in mind.

We collect nothing else. There is no account to create, and you can read the whole site without sending us anything.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry.

Your details are used to respond to your enquiry and to continue the conversation about a possible commission. That is the only purpose.

We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies, no tracking.

This website sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers. We do not build a profile of your visit.

External requests

Fonts from Google.

To display the typefaces Cormorant Garamond and Manrope, this site requests them from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google's servers, which — like any web request — can see your IP address. If the fonts are unavailable, the page falls back to system type and still works.

Keeping it, and your choices

Retention and control.

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any conversation that follows. You can ask us to correct or delete what you have sent at any time.

To make a request, use the enquiry form and tell us what you would like us to do.

In short

The summary.

  • We collect only what you send through the enquiry form.
  • We use it solely to reply to you.
  • No cookies, analytics, or trackers.
  • One external request, for fonts.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time.
