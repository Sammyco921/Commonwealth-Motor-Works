01 · The difference

A different starting point.

Most automobiles begin with a model. A Commonwealth begins with a person.

What matters to you determines the direction: the way you want a car to feel, the places it should take you, the details you notice, and the ideas you want to bring to life.

Our role is to help you discover what that automobile could be, and to develop it with the care, imagination, and engineering judgment required to make it real.

02 · Three equal parts

Vision, collaboration, automobile.

I

Your vision

Every commission begins with what matters to you. Your ideas, preferences, and intended experience establish the direction.

II

Our collaboration

You bring the perspective. We bring design, engineering, and creative judgment. Together, we shape the automobile.

III

The finished automobile

Every decision must contribute to the whole: a coherent design, considered engineering, and a car that delivers on the vision behind it.

Our principle

A change in direction is a conversation, not a reason to feel guilty.

03 · The commissioning experience

Made together, from the beginning.

Six stages, from the first conversation to handover.

Explore the Commissioning Process
  1. ConversationDiscover the customer's vision, needs, tastes, and ambitions.
  2. DirectionExplore possibilities and develop a shared creative direction.
  3. DevelopmentEvaluate design and engineering options, resolve constraints, and refine the proposal.

04 · Design

Distinctly yours. Entirely considered.

Personalization is not a list of additions. It is the process of deciding what belongs.

Proportion, material, color, ergonomics, mechanical character, and intended use must work together. We help you explore possibilities, challenge assumptions when necessary, and refine the details until the automobile feels unmistakably yours.

Explore Our Design Philosophy

Proportion, material, color, ergonomics.

05 · Engineering

Imagination, made accountable.

An ambitious idea deserves more than an attractive rendering.

Every commission must be considered as a complete automobile. Design intent, component selection, manufacturability, integration, validation, serviceability, and long-term ownership all matter.

Originality should earn its complexity. Where established solutions serve the vision well, they deserve consideration. Where a different solution is necessary, it must justify itself.

06 · The demonstrator

An expression of the Commonwealth approach.

The first demonstrator explores what Commonwealth stands for in a single automobile: grand-touring proportions, contemporary design, and an approach guided by the complete vehicle rather than a single headline specification.

It is the beginning of a design philosophy made tangible, not a template that every future commission must follow.

Discover the Demonstrator

07 · Ownership

The relationship doesn't end at delivery.

An exceptional automobile deserves thoughtful care throughout its life.

Commonwealth's ownership relationship extends beyond the handover, with a point of contact for maintenance, repairs, technical questions, parts support, and the preservation of each automobile's records.

The aim is simple: to remain involved in the care of the car we helped create.
