01 · The difference

A more personal beginning.

A Commonwealth begins not with a catalogue, but with a conversation.

We want to understand what matters to you: the way you like to travel, the details you notice, and the feeling you hope to find behind the wheel.

From there, we explore what the automobile might become—thoughtfully, collaboratively, and with care for the whole.

02 · A shared vision

Considered from every angle.

I

Your point of view

Your interests, instincts, and aspirations give the commission its character and direction.

II

A thoughtful collaboration

You bring what matters to you. We bring design, engineering, and a considered perspective. The work takes shape together.

III

A complete automobile

Each decision belongs to the same whole: an automobile with coherence, purpose, and a character of its own.

Our principle

A change in direction is part of the conversation.

03 · The commissioning experience

From first thoughts to first drive.

A considered process, shaped around the automobile and the person who will own it.

Explore the Commissioning Process
  1. ConversationWe begin by learning what you value, how you travel, and what you imagine.
  2. DirectionTogether, we find a creative direction that feels true to your vision.
  3. DevelopmentWe explore the possibilities, consider the practicalities, and refine the proposal.
  4. SpecificationWe bring the choices into focus, from materials and details to scope and intent.
  5. CreationDesign, engineering, and specialist making come together in the development of the automobile.
  6. HandoverThe finished automobile is presented, and the relationship continues into ownership.

04 · Design

Personal, by design.

True distinction is rarely a matter of adding more. It comes from knowing what belongs.

Form, materials, color, comfort, and mechanical character are considered together. We’ll help you explore the possibilities and shape the details into an automobile that feels unmistakably yours.

Explore Our Design Philosophy

Form, material, touch, and proportion.

05 · Engineering

Thought through, down to the detail.

An idea becomes meaningful when it works beautifully as an automobile.

Design intent, materials, integration, manufacture, and long-term care all deserve attention. We consider how each decision contributes to the whole, and how the automobile will feel to live with over time.

Where simplicity serves the vision, we value it. Where a more distinctive solution is called for, it must earn its place.

06 · The demonstrator

A first expression of the idea.

The demonstrator brings the Commonwealth approach into view: grand-touring proportions, contemporary design, and a focus on the character of the complete automobile.

It is a beginning, not a template. Each future commission will have its own character and direction.

07 · Ownership

Considered beyond the handover.

The experience of an automobile continues long after its first journey.

We intend to remain a thoughtful point of contact for care, maintenance, technical questions, parts, and the records that accompany each car.

It is a continuation of the care that begins with the commission.
