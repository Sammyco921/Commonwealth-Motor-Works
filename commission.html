The Commission

Your vision, thoughtfully realised.

A commission may begin with a carefully formed brief, or with no more than a feeling. Together, we give that first idea room to take shape—one considered decision at a time.

Stage 01

Conversation

We begin by understanding what you imagine, and what matters most to you.

We begin withYour ideas, in whatever form they take: a feeling, a purpose, a reference.

The conversationWe listen, ask questions, and start to understand what matters most to you.

What takes shapeA shared sense of what the automobile should be and the life it should suit.

Stage 02

Direction

We explore possibilities and find a direction that feels distinctly yours.

We exploreEarly design studies, material references, and possibilities that express your ideas.

Your perspectiveWhat resonates, what surprises you, and what doesn’t feel quite right helps guide the work.

Where we arriveA creative direction that feels worth developing together.

Stage 03

Development

Design and engineering come together as the proposal is refined.

We considerHow the design and engineering can work together as a complete automobile.

Along the wayWe explain the possibilities, constraints, and trade-offs as they arise.

What takes shapeA refined proposal, grounded in what can be realised.

Stage 04

Specification

The details are brought together into a clear, agreed brief.

The detailsMaterials, features, scope, and other choices are brought together in one clear brief.

Before proceedingYou have the opportunity to review the specification, ask questions, and approve it.

What it providesA shared understanding of the automobile we intend to create.

Stage 05

Creation

The automobile takes shape through coordinated design, engineering, and specialist making.

The workDesign, engineering, and specialist making come together as the automobile takes shape.

Staying connectedYour AD keeps you informed and involved at the moments when your perspective matters.

If something changesWe’ll explain the implications of a proposed change before proceeding.

Stage 06

Handover

A personal introduction to the finished automobile, and the beginning of ownership.

The presentationA personal introduction to the finished automobile and the details that make it yours.

What accompanies itThe relevant engineering, maintenance, and ownership documentation.

What comes nextThe beginning of ownership, with your AD remaining a point of contact.

Your AD

A familiar point of contact.

Each customer has a dedicated AD: a primary point of contact throughout the commission and into ownership.

Your AD keeps the conversation connected, understands the decisions behind your automobile, and helps coordinate the people and details involved along the way.

Changing your mind

There is room to reconsider.

A commission develops through conversation. If your thinking changes along the way, we’ll talk it through with you.

A change may affect the work already completed, the materials involved, or the schedule. If there are cost or timing implications, we’ll explain them before any additional chargeable work begins. Nothing proceeds on a revised basis until you understand the implications and approve the change.

The aim is to make each decision clear, considered, and shared.

Common questions

A few things you may be wondering.

Do I need a finished design?

No. You may arrive with sketches and references, or with only a sense of how you would like the automobile to feel. We can begin there.

Will you help me understand what is feasible?

Yes. We’ll explain the considerations behind an idea and, where needed, explore alternatives that better serve the vision.

Do I need to share a budget at the outset?

Not necessarily. We can discuss budget when it becomes useful, as the direction and scope of the commission take shape.

What if my ideas change?

That’s part of a collaborative process. We’ll talk through any cost or schedule implications before additional chargeable work proceeds.

Personal elements of the experience

A record of the journey.

  • The commissioning letterWhen a commission is formally agreed, a personal letter introduces your AD and sets out the next steps.
  • The design archiveA curated physical record of selected design work, approved decisions, material choices, and the automobile’s development.
  • The scale modelA carefully developed scale model accompanies the completed automobile.

At handover, the automobile remains at the centre. Engineering, maintenance, and ownership documents are presented separately, with care and clarity.
