Design Philosophy

A car should reflect the person it was created for.

Personal expression is only part of the challenge. A successful automobile brings its elements together: exterior, interior, materials, mechanical character, ergonomics, and intended use should reinforce one another.

Design as collaboration

We don't simply execute every request.

Our designers and engineers contribute ideas, identify conflicts, offer alternatives, and help you discover possibilities you might not have considered. Your vision stays central; we contribute the expertise to refine it. The relationship is collaborative, not hierarchical.

The right decisions for this automobile.

Distinct commissions

Two customers, two very different cars.

Results may differ radically in character. Commonwealth's identity lives in the quality of the process, the care of the decisions, and the integrity of the automobile, not in forcing every commission into one visual formula.

More customization does not automatically mean better design.

The purpose isn't to add as many features as possible. It is to make the right decisions for this particular automobile.
